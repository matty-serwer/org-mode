* Pages & Sections
** Home (Landing Page:/)
Goal:Introduce your company and immediately spotlight "Phantom Power".
- Hero Section:
  - LuupTechDesign logo/name
  - Tagline/one-liner (e.g. “Building smart, impactful web applications”)
  - Eye-catching “Phantom Power” feature (image, short description, call-to-action)
- What We Do/Our Mission:
  - Brief company vision and story—what sets you apart, your values
- Featured Application:
  - Short cards/teasers for Phantom Power and other key apps
  - Button/link to “Learn More” (Applications page, or straight to /applications/phantom-power)
- Quick Links:
  - CTA buttons: Hire Us, Explore Phantom Power, See Our Work, Contact
- Footer:
  - Social links, contact info, copyright
** About (/about)
Goal:Company background + your personal/professional bio.
- Company Story:
  - What is LuupTechDesign and why does it exist? (Vision/mission, how you started, what you want to achieve)
- Founder Bio:
  - Your picture, short bio, key skills, highlights from your career
  - Optional: “Values” or “Philosophy” (why you build this way)
- Company Timeline/Key Milestones
- Option to Add Team/Contributors Later
** Applications (/applications)
Goal:Overview of flagship product(s) and other applications.
- Phantom Power (as featured application):
  - App icon/logo, tagline (e.g. “The modern power automation platform”)
  - 2-3 sentence elevator pitch
  - CTA: “Learn more” (to/applications/phantom-power)
- Other Applications:
  - Brief list/cards for Rutherford Diagnostics, Other Apps, etc.
  - Each with CTA (to subpage or modal)
** Phantom Power Application Feature (/applications/phantom-power)
Goal:Dedicated landing page for Phantom Power—pitch to stakeholders, users, and partners.
- What is Phantom Power?
  - Extended pitch, vision, and what problems it solves
- Key Features/Benefits:
  - Bulleted list or icons/brief descriptions
- Screenshots/Demo:
  - Images, GIFs, or (future) video
- Product Roadmap/Status:
  - What’s live, what’s coming, invite to collaborate/partner
- Call to Action:
  - “Get in Touch,” “Request Access,” “Join as Stakeholder,” etc.
** Work/Projects (/work)
Goal:Past work, client projects, case studies.
- Headline:
  - “Selected Projects” or “Our Work”
- Featured Case Studies or Client Work:
  - Cards for contract projects, freelance work, impressive company roles, etc.
  - If limited, focus on quality, impact, or learning
- Optional Testimonials:
  - Quotes from collaborators or clients (add as you get them!)
** Services (optional) (/services)
Goal:What you/your company offer—consulting, development, etc.
- Service List:
  - Web Application Development
  - Tech Consulting
  - Prototyping, Product Strategy
  - Custom Services
- How to Engage:
  - How you work, engagement models, contact for estimate/discovery call
** Blog (/blog)
Goal:Share expertise, product updates, technical writeups, company news.
- Blog Index:
  - List or cards for posts
  - Mix product dev updates, explainers, industry commentary, milestone announcements
- Individual Posts:
  - MDX page(s) for content
** Resume / For Recruiters (/resume)
Goal:Enable recruiters/hiring managers to quickly access your CV and critical info.
- Intro:
  - “Open to Opportunities,” short professional statement
- Downloadable Resume:
  - PDF or online embed
- Skills & Experience Summary:
  - Clean, scannable bullets or grid
- Contact Info:
  - Email, LinkedIn
** Contact (/contact)
Goal:Simple, barrier-free way to start a conversation.
- Contact Form:
  - Name, Email, Message
- Direct Email/Links:
  - Your email, LinkedIn, Twitter, etc.
General Page Sections/Components Used Throughout:
- Navbar:
  - Logo, main links (Home, Applications, About, Resume, Contact, [Work/Blog])
** Footer:
- Social icons, copyright
** Project/App Cards:
- For quick teasers of what you’ve built
