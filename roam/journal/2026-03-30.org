Since rayon is a delicate fabric, you'll want to treat it gently — no hot water, no aggressive scrubbing, and always test any solution on a hidden seam first. Here's a prioritized outline using what you have plus a couple of alternatives:[1]
** Using What You Have
- *Micellar/cellular water first* — Dab directly onto the stain with a cotton pad, blotting (never rubbing) from the outside in; great as a first pass since it's designed to lift makeup pigment[2]
- *Dish soap soak* — Apply a small amount directly to the stain, work it in gently with your fingers, let sit 15–20 minutes, then rinse with *cold* water (hot water sets stains deeper into rayon fibers)[3][4]
- *Baking soda paste* — Mix with a little cold water into a paste, apply to the stain, let sit 10–15 minutes to absorb oils, then brush off gently and rinse[5]
** Handy Alternatives to Grab
- *Rubbing alcohol* — Dampen a cotton ball and blot the stain; especially effective on set/dried foundation; test on a hidden seam first since rayon can be sensitive[6][1]
- *White shaving cream* — Apply, let sit ~10 minutes, rinse cold; works surprisingly well on pigment-heavy rouge/blush[7][2]
- *Oil-free makeup remover* — If you have any around, it's tailor-made to dissolve foundation chemistry and is gentle enough for delicate fabrics[1]
** Key Rules for Rayon
- Always use *cold water only* — heat sets the stain and can warp rayon[4]
- *Blot, don't rub* — rubbing pushes pigment deeper into the fibers[2]
- Air dry only after treating — do not machine dry until you're sure the stain is fully out[1]
- If the stain still won't budge after all this, rayon is one of those fabrics where a *professional dry cleaner* is worth the trip rather than risking damage[7]
