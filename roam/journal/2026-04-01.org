  - Link Audio
    - Live 12.4 adds *Link Audio*, letting you and your peers stream audio between Link Audio-enabled devices on a local network in real time
    - Audio from other players appears directly as an input in Live
      - Monitor and record external devices and apps without additional hardware, cables, or manual latency compensation
    - Read more about how Link Audio works on Push, Move and Note below
  - Updated devices
    - Erosion
      - Live's classic signal degradation effect, now available on Move and Note for the first time
      - Gets a revamp on Live and Push
        - Real-time spectrum visualization
        - Ability to smoothly blend between sine and noise modulation
        - Ability to smoothly blend between mono and stereo noise
      - Updates come in the form of a new device
      - The previous version, now named Erosion Legacy, will be accessible in older Live Sets
    - [[https://reflect-assets.app/v1/users/IOPbOsCY3ngO02wCaCgY0oSjlTM2/0885471aac5b45a5a374c94c90a69835/8c593aa1-50a0-4bd2-b8d8-7ec875304636?key=7484908b1d4b541384b3cf75e105b5de67b167225445eadcf61205893dad3b4b][image]]

      - Makes it easier to create smooth, musical chorus sounds
        - Especially effective on guitar and bass
