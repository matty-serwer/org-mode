- [[id:a2ed4822-b4be-4ca0-acab-e4e59abe45c1][Job Interview]] Notes 
  [[https://www.youtube-nocookie.com/embed/T__1QViXUxk][youtube: https://www.youtube-nocookie.com/embed/T__1QViXUxk]]
- "How Are You?"
  - You're always "Great!, How are you?"
  - This is a test on how well you are regulated for big moments
- "Why do you want to work here?"
  - The answer should tell them about what YOU can do for THEM
  - "I've outgrown my current job, I'm looking for a specific challenge. Based on what I've seen about this team's goals, I know I can help you solve X."
- We're shifting the energy from audition, to a meeting of TWO parties that have something to contribute to each other
- "Where do you see yourself in 5 years?"
  - "I see myself here, I see myself becoming an expert in this area and a valuable part of this team. This role aligns with where I want to go because..." Then connect with something specific about the job.
- "Why are you looking for a new opportunity?"
  - Nothing negative. Don't be a risk.
  - "I've outgrown my current role, and I'm looking for a new challenge"
- "How do you feel about your current manager?"
  - Say nothing about your co-workers, nothing about leadership, nothing negative ever. Ew. But you don't know who's interviewing you, or what their style is.
  - Turn it instead into positive
  - Something positive about manager > Something you got out of it > Bring that something to the new company. 
- "Hobbies?"
  - Be memmorable. Give them something to work with.
  - I make shoegazey electronic music. I recently started acting at the neighborhood theater. I do in-home pet sitting. I love to cook. 
- "Your job title and description?"
  - Claim work you do above your paygrade.
  - Don't wait for title or permission.
  - "While my title was X, I did Z. This required me to ..."
- Resume Gaps
  - Don't stress!
  - They saw it. You still got the interview, so it's not a deal breaker
  - Just don't make it weird! Don't be defensive or appologetic.
  - Follow up with how you kept your skills sharp.
- "Do you have any questions fo us?"
  - Don't say no!
    - Communicates that you're not interested, haven't thought beyond today, and are just trying to get through this.
  - Have at least 2, 3 questions ready
    - Why is this position open?
    - What would you consider to be successful in the first 3 and 6 months in this position.
    - Can you tell me a little about the dynamic between this team and the teams they generally collaborate with?
    - What do you think is the most immediate challange the person in this role would need to tackle in their first month or so?
  - Don't negotiate (remote work. vacation)
- [[id:3eca63c6-f7fb-4416-bb70-6e5a767112d4][Time Management]] 
  [[https://www.youtube-nocookie.com/embed/KTOVuUKIHVw][youtube: https://www.youtube-nocookie.com/embed/KTOVuUKIHVw]]
  - When you wake up, brain dump
    - Get everything out of your brain until it’s empty
  - Todo meditation
    - 10 minute meditation with a pen and paper
    - If something pops into your head, write it down sloppily with your eyes closed.
    - After, add to todo list
  - Prioritize the list
    - Circle the top 3
    - Put those 3 on blast
    - They get done first
  - Master Your Schedule
    - Write it out
    - Time Blocking
      1. Block out unmovable things.
        - Meetings
        - Appointments
        - Calls
      1. Look at the remaining things on the todo list and add them in
        - Eating
        - Working Out
        - Planning
        - Catch Ups
        - Code Block
        - Guitar Riff
