  *Auto-Prevent Task Overlap with Configurable Buffer Setting*
  /Building on previous overlap management requests with a specific ADHD-friendly solution/
  -----
  
  *The Problem*
  The app has great drag-and-drop motion and /almost/ has the logic right—tasks bump out of the way when you drag between them. But after a few adjustments, I end up with 5-6 overlapping tasks showing "Tasks are *overlapping*" flags everywhere.
  *Clicking "overlapping" doesn't fix anything.* It just opens the full task editor where you're adjusting times blind, can't see what you're conflicting with, and it's nearly impossible to resolve without starting over.
  For ADHD time-blocking workflows, this breaks the entire experience. If it's not easy in a brand new way, I can't get through all the "what-ifs" suggested by these seemingly ‘automatic errors’ without abandoning structured time-blocking entirely. And I know, there are ADHD-centric apps, but I think this style of hangup might affect up to 50% of all users to some degree. 
  -----
  
  *Proposed Solution*
  Let users set a default gap between tasks:
  *Benefits:*
  This transforms drag behavior from creating problems into maintaining realistic, achievable schedules with built-in breathing room.
  -----
  
  *Does this already exist somewhere? If not, it would make Structured genuinely usable for ADHD users who desperately want time-blocking to work. For me the app is otherwise gorgeous and beautiful. *
  - *Dragging tasks creates cascading overlaps instead of preventing them.*
  - *Add a configurable overlap tolerance/buffer setting.*
  - *Current behavior:* Allows ~10min overlap (represents an impossibility)
  - *Proposed:* Allow *-5min or -10min buffer* (negative overlap = mandatory gap)
  - ✅ Prevents impossible scheduling automatically
  - ✅ Accounts for transition time, bathroom breaks, context switching
  - ✅ Eliminates the cascading overlap problem entirely
  - ✅ Reduces cognitive load and uncertainty
