The snow would come. It always came. He did not think about the snow.

The buses moved through it because buses move through things, and the planes rose when they rose. He would board the one with an open door. That was all a man could do — find the open door and walk through it.

He had done harder things than this. By night the air would be warm and thick in the way Florida air is warm and thick, and Eric would be there, and they would shake hands the way men shake hands when real work is about to begin. He was not afraid of the work. He had never been afraid of the work.

The snow was already behind him.
