* [[id:b4a0831f-116c-439b-b18f-6f3b7ead03ee][Medical]] Volunteer Index of [[id:a766647b-b1b4-4162-a667-6b54b1d2ae96][Terminology]]
*** Core Terms
- *Volunteer* *Physician*
  - A licensed medical doctor who offers services without compensation, often in underserved or crisis-affected areas.
- *Medical Mission*
  - A temporary deployment of healthcare providers to deliver medical care, usually coordinated by a nonprofit or religious organization.
- *Humanitarian Aid*
  - Material or logistical assistance provided for humanitarian purposes, typically in response to crises including natural disasters or conflict.
- *Global Health*
  - A field of study, research, and practice that places a priority on improving health and achieving equity in health for all people worldwide.
- *Non-Governmental Organization (NGO)*
  - An independent group that operates aid programs in global health and disaster relief.
*** Program Types
- *Short-Term Medical Mission (STMM)*
  - A project in which medical volunteers work abroad for a brief period (from a week up to a few months).
- *Long-Term Placement*
  - Assignments lasting several months to years, often focusing on sustainable impact and capacity building.
- *Disaster Response Team*
  - A group of specialists deployed rapidly to provide immediate care during emergencies (e.g., after earthquakes or epidemics).
*** Permitting and Credentialing
- *Licensure*
  - Verification that a medical provider is licensed to practice in their home country; often required by host countries.
- *Temporary Registration*
  - Provision by a host nation for short-term licensure of international medical volunteers.
- *Credentialing*
  - The process of validating a physician's qualifications and professional background by host organizations.
*** Ethical and Legal Considerations
- *Scope of Practice*
  - The procedures, actions, and processes permitted by law for a particular health professional.
- *Informed Consent*
  - The process of providing patients with relevant information to make informed healthcare decisions.
- *Cultural Competence*
  - The ability to understand, communicate with, and effectively interact with people across cultures.
- *Medical Liability*
  - Legal responsibility incurred by physicians for patient outcomes during volunteer work.
*** Organizational & Financial Terms
- *Sponsoring Organization*
  - The group (e.g., NGO, church, foundation) that organizes and supports volunteers.
- *In-Country Partner*
  - Local organization or healthcare facility collaborating with international volunteers.
- *Cost-Sharing*
  - Financial model where expenses (travel, lodging) are divided between volunteers and sponsors.
- *Tax Deductible*
  - Expenses that may be claimed as deductions under IRS rules for charitable work.
*** Popular Organizations
- *Doctors Without Borders (Médecins Sans Frontières/MSF)*
  - International medical humanitarian organization.
- *Operation Smile*
  - Provides free cleft lip and palate surgery in low-resource countries.
- *Project HOPE*
  - Provides medical training and health education globally.
- *Partners In Health*
  - Focuses on providing a preferential option for the poor in health care.
