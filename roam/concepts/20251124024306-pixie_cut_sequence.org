* Pixie cut
- Crop up the top front and top back

- Cut the back bottom half short, one finger in the middle and then straight out from there
- [[https://reflect-assets.app/v1/users/IOPbOsCY3ngO02wCaCgY0oSjlTM2/fe8daa60ec7640a0a5ac42ff5f25ba82/d2deb07e-2ae2-410f-bd2d-792458d9bb56?key=df91652d040d7d919c123bc3b8219b96f305c7f0e0e50bf2cc838fa40c1c29b3][image]]
- Cut around ears and neck
- Let one layer of back top down
  - Twist into 3 sections
  - Cut the middle about an inch about 45 degrees down (less in our case)
  - Cut out from there pulling hair in to use the middle as a guide
    - This results In hair getting longer as we go out
- Next later down in back
  - Twist into 7 
  - Cut center about 2 inches at 45 degrees
- [[https://reflect-assets.app/v1/users/IOPbOsCY3ngO02wCaCgY0oSjlTM2/2d82fcb9b1d94800bb637abb845f7164/9425c302-0c49-4e8b-8ae3-c3503cfd0ff7?key=b7c8c44b3dc406fdc077261503191a9b79cb3506e0903b54f870131a13d073c4][image]]
- Next later 5
- Then 3 so that top is the longest
* Front half
- Twist and tie most of it to the right, leaving one layer down on the left side
- Cut in 45 degree down even cuts, should be length of the top of the back

- Do two thin layers on the left side
- Then do same in right side with two layers
