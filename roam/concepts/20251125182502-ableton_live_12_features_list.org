#+title: Ableton Live 12 Features
#+filetags: :concept:list:
#+date: 2025-11-25
* [[id:67889323-a4c5-48d8-91eb-d44995ce8dff][Ableton Live]] 12 Features
*1. *[[id:f5c88680-5509-42dc-bef9-bd8705c9313b][Ableton 12 MIDI Tools]]
- *MIDI Generators (Rhythm, Seed, Shape, Stacks, Euclidean)*
-
  - Suite for procedural note/chord/rhythm creation within selections.
- *MIDI Transformations (Arpeggiate, Chop, Connect, Glissando, LFO, Ornament, Quantize, Recombine, Span, Strum, Time Warp, Velocity Shaper)*
-
  - Effects to evolve existing patterns with timing, velocity, and articulation tweaks.
- *Find and Select Notes*
-
  - Filter MIDI by pitch, velocity, duration, etc., for precise editing.
- *Scale Awareness*
-
  - Global scale snapping/highlighting for tools and clips.
- *Chop MIDI Tool*
-
  - Advanced slicing with patterns and randomization.
- *Apply Grooves Instantly*
-
  - Auto-load grooves on new clips with global control.
*2. Audio Processing & Manipulation (Sample Handling and FX)*
- *Stem Separation*
-
  - AI splits audio into Vocals/Drums/Bass/Other tracks.
- *Roar Saturator*
-
  - Dynamic distortion with modes like Delay Routing and MIDI sidechain.
- *Auto Shift*
-
  - Real-time pitch correction with formant/vibrato and scale support.
- *Bounce Tracks/Groups to Audio*
-
  - Render selections in-place or to new clips/tracks.
- *Limiter Overhaul*
-
  - Redesigned with Mid/Side, Soft Clip, and Gain Reduction Link.
- *Saturator Redesign*
-
  - Updated curves, Bass Enhancer, and EQ view.
- *Auto Filter Redesign*
-
  - Stereo mod, new filter types (DJ/Comb), and sidechain EQ.
- *Auto Pan-Tremolo*
-
  - Expanded modes with LFO shapes and frequency modulation.
- *Echo Updates*
-
  - Smoother repitch and delay calcs.
- *Envelope Follower*
-
  - Added sidechain and Attack Hold.
*3. Instruments & Synths (Sound Generation)*
- *Meld Synthesizer*
-
  - Bi-timbral MPE synth with modulation, chords, and evolving textures.
- *Drum Sampler*
-
  - One-shot rack instrument with envelopes, filters, and velocity mod.
- *Granulator III*
-
  - Updated granular synth with envelopes and MIDI mapping.
- *Operator Updates*
-
  - Per-note pitch bend and 32-voice polyphony.
- *Drift*
-
  - Hi-Quality mode for aliasing reduction.
- *Tuner*
-
  - Tuning system support with reference adjustments.
*4. Browser & Integration (Discovery and Asset Management)*
- *Splice Integration*
-
  - In-browser search, preview, download, and “Search with Sound.”
- *Similarity Search and Swapping*
-
  - AI finds/swaps similar samples/presets in devices.
- *Auto Tagging*
-
  - Automatic labels for short samples.
- *Full-Height Browser*
-
  - Expandable view for larger previews.
- *Browser Redesign*
-
  - Tag filters, Quick Tags, sortable results, and MIDI filters.
- *Tuning Systems*
-
  - Per-track alternative tunings with browser integration.
- *New Packs*
-
  - Orchestral Strings, Chop and Swing, plus Splice/DECAP samples.
- *Core Library Refinements*
-
  - Updated Drum Racks, presets, and tuning templates.
*5. UI & Workflow (Navigation and Efficiency)*
- *Device A/B Comparison*
-
  - Toggle/compare device states for tweaking.
- *Undo History*
-
  - List-based multi-step recovery.
- *Mixer in Arrangement View*
-
  - Toggleable panel with returns and navigation.
- *Arrangement View Enhancements*
-
  - Vertical zoom, clip insertion, and comping.
- *Session View Updates*
-
  - Scene Follow Actions and clip dragging.
- *Clip View Tweaks*
-
  - Release Velocity lane and audio “Set 1.1.1 Here.”
- *MIDI Editing Improvements*
-
  - Shortcuts for split/join, selection, and zoom.
- *Accessibility Enhancements*
-
  - Screen reader support and UI zoom.
- *CC Control*
-
  - MIDI CC utility with learn and banks.
*6. Performance & Extensibility (Under-the-Hood and Customization)*
- *Max for Live MIDI Tools*
-
  - Framework for custom Generators/Transformations.
- *Control Surface Support*
-
  - New for Akai MPK, Novation Launch, Arturia KeyLab.
- *Performance Tweaks*
-
  - Faster loads, GPU rendering, and CPU/memory optimizations.
