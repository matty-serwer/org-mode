#+title: Context (Note)
#+filetags: :concept:
#+date: 2025-10-26
/Add Personal Context an Emotion to a Note/
1. *Why *was this item created? action taken? note created?
1. *What *is your physical context? 
1. *How *do you feel about this topic?
- Notes:
  - any or all three can be relevant
  - The idea being that we will struggle to place notes as time goes by. 
  - Emotion is semi-optional, and can range from 'optimistic' or 'work mode' to 'I was devistate'
- Practice:
  - Personal Context should be at the bottom of a note
  - Use backlinks, as tags are [[id:dbd1104c-49a2-4462-ab1e-67d872860321][Note Scope]]
  - Header is = - [[Personal Context]] =
  - Body in lace in quote block
- Example: 
  - Personal Context
