* [[id:8fee8dc9-268c-4ee9-9572-4da77ef34489][Resume]]  for [[id:fbdd7685-ffb3-47da-8689-477255745edc][Preble Street]] *Wellness Shelter Client Support Staff*
- Why a Specialized Resume Makes Sense
  - Different evaluation criteria
    - A halfway house isn't evaluating you on GitHub contributions or technical architecture decisions. They're looking for:
      - Reliability and trustworthiness
      - Interpersonal skills and empathy
      - Ability to handle crisis situations calmly
      - Consistency and follow-through
      - Community-oriented mindset
    - Your tech resume emphasizes things that don't matter to them (and might even raise concerns—why would a programmer take a $23/hour job?).
  - How to Reframe Your Experience
    - Your hostel management is GOLD for this application:
      - You managed vulnerable populations (travelers, often in unfamiliar situations)
      - You handled conflict resolution, safety concerns, and rule enforcement
      - You demonstrated reliability (2-year commitment shows stability)
      - You built community and helped people navigate difficult transitions
      - You managed operations, budgets, and logistics
    - Restaurant/bar experience shows:
      - Ability to work under pressure
      - Customer service and de-escalation skills
      - Following protocols and procedures
      - Teamwork in high-stress environments
  - Resume Structure for This Role
    - Instead of "Technical Skills" and "Projects," organize around:
      - Summary
        - Your genuine interest in helping others, stability, reliability
      - Relevant Experience
        - Hostel management (lead with this), restaurant/bar roles
      - Skills
        - Communication, problem-solving, crisis management, attention to detail, reliability, empathy
      - Education
        - Include it, but don't lead with it
  - Specific Language to Use
    - Instead of: "Managed hostel operations"
    - Try: "Provided safe, supportive environment for 50+ residents; resolved conflicts and enforced house policies while maintaining compassionate approach"
    - Instead of: "Worked in fast-paced restaurant"
    - Try: "Remained calm and professional during high-stress situations; supported team members and guests through challenging moments"
  - What NOT to Do
    - Don't mention you're "between jobs" or frame it as temporary
    - Don't emphasize the study-while-working benefit (frame it as: you value continuous learning and self-improvement)
    - Don't include a lengthy tech background unless it's relevant (like if they need basic computer skills)
  - The Cover Letter is Your MVP
    - This is where you explain /why/ you're genuinely interested in this work. Be honest: What draws you to community service? What did you learn from the hostel experience that shaped your values? Why now? This matters more than the resume itself.
    - The fact that you're asking this question thoughtfully suggests you'll be good at this work. Good luck! 🙌
