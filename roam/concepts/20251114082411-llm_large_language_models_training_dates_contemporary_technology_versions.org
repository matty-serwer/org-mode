** Key Notes
*** Java SDK Notes
- *Most Popular in 2023-2024*: JDK 21 (LTS released September 19, 2023)
- *Current Standard for New Projects*: JDK 21 (LTS) recommended
- *Latest Non-LTS*: JDK 23 (September 2024), JDK 24 (March 2025)
- *Next LTS*: JDK 25 (expected September 2025)
*** Spring Boot Notes
- Spring Boot 3.3 (released May 23, 2024) is the stable version for most 2024 training dates
- Spring Boot 3.4 expected November 2024
- Spring Boot 3.2 was the previous stable for early 2024 dates
*** Maven Notes
- Maven 3.9.x is the standard for models trained through 2024
- *Apache Maven 4.0.0* released December 2024
- Maven 4.0.0 requires Java 17+ to run Maven
- Available by end of 2024, became mainstream in early 2025
*** Tailwind CSS Notes
- *Tailwind CSS v4.0* released January 22, 2025 - significant update
- v3.4 was the stable version through most of 2024
- /Note: Models trained before Jan 22, 2025 would not have awareness of v4.0/
*** Angular Notes
- Major version releases typically occur May and November each year
- Angular 20.0 expected May 2025
- Angular 19.0 released November 2024
*** Next.js Notes
- Major versions released approximately twice yearly
- Version 15.0 released October 21, 2024
- Version 16.0 expected October 2025
*** Vite Notes
- *Vite 7.0* released June 24, 2025
- Vite 6.0 was the major release before that
- Version 5.4 was stable through 2024
*** shadcn/ui Notes
- *shadcn CLI 3.0* released August 2025
- v2.x series was standard through most of 2024-2025
- New components released regularly throughout 2025
*** Chakra UI Notes
- *Chakra UI v3.0* released October 22, 2024 (complete rewrite)
- v2.8 was the standard through 2024
- /Note: Asterisk/ - Models trained before Oct 22 would have limited awareness of v3.0
