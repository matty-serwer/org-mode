*Your honest answer*:

**** 3. "How do you handle someone who's escalating?"
*Your framework* (this is gold):
1. *Assess*: Is anyone in immediate danger? What's driving this?
1. *Listen*: Often people escalate because they feel unheard
1. *De-escalate*: Lower your voice, create space, avoid defensive posture
1. *Offer choices*: When possible, give the person agency
1. *Know your limits*: When to call for backup or professional support
*Use a real example* from your stories above.

*Your strongest selling point*:
- Six years of overnight key-holding with perfect attendance

**** 6. "Tell us about a time you had to work with a difficult team member."

- Focus on collaboration, not complaint

- Not requiring people to earn dignity or basic respect
- Understanding triggers and creating predictable, safe environments
