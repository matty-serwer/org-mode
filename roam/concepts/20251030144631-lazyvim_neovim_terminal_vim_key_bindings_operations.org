** Built-in Neovim Commands
- =:tabnew= - Opens a new tab
- =:split= - Opens a horizontal split
- =:vsplit= - Opens a vertical split
- =:terminal= - Opens a terminal in the current window
** LazyVim's Default Keybindings
=<leader>ft= - Often mapped to open a floating terminal
=<leader>tn= - Often mapped to open a new terminal tab (config dependent)
** Common Approach: Terminal with Split
=:vsplit=
:=terminal=
- Or combined:
  :=vsplit | terminal=
** Finding Your Keybindings
Check your config files at:
=~/.config/nvim/lua/config/keymaps.lua=
- Or list all mappings:
  :=map=
** Toggleterm Plugin (if installed)
=<leader>tf= - Toggle floating terminal
=<leader>tv= - Toggle vertical terminal
=<leader>th= - Toggle horizontal terminal
