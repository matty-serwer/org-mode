#+title: Facial Skin Routine
#+filetags: :concept:
#+date: 2026-04-23
** Daily
*Every Morning*
- CeraVe Hydrating Cleanser
- The Ordinary Niacinamide 10% + Zinc
- VaniCream
- EltaMD UV Clear SPF 46
*Every Evening (non-IPL days)*
- Shave
- CeraVe Hydrating Cleanser
- The Ordinary Azelaic Acid Suspension 10%
- VaniCream
*Every Other Evening (IPL days)*
- Shave close
- Do Ulike session
- CeraVe Hydrating Cleanser
- VaniCream only — no actives tonight
*2-3x per week (non-IPL evenings only)*
- Swap azelaic acid for The Ordinary Glycolic Acid 7% on a cotton pad
- VaniCream
-----

** Individual
*Niacinamide 10% + Zinc — every morning after cleansing*
- Apply a few drops to dry face
- Pat in, don't rub
- Moisturizer goes on top
-----

*Azelaic Acid 10% — every non-IPL evening after cleansing*
- Pump a small amount onto fingertips
- Spread evenly across face
- VaniCream goes on top
-----

*Glycolic Acid 7% — 2-3x per week, non-IPL evenings, replaces azelaic acid*
- Soak a cotton pad
- Swipe across face, avoid eye area
- Wait 5 minutes
- VaniCream goes on top
