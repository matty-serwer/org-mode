#+title: Ableton 12 MIDI Tools
#+filetags: :concept:
#+date: 2025-11-25
*MIDI Generators*
- *Rhythm*
-
  - Generates a note pattern according to set parameters, repeated to fill a given time selection.
- *Seed*
-
  - Randomly generates notes within specified pitch, length, and velocity ranges, with controls for the number of simultaneous notes and overall note count.
- *Shape*
-
  - Generates a sequence of notes within a range of pitches, distributed following a user-defined shape in the tool’s display.
- *Stacks*
-
  - Adds individual chords or creates chord progressions within a selected scale, filling the time selection or loop length.
- *Euclidean*
-
  - Generates notes based on Euclidean rhythms for up to four voices at a time, within the time selection or loop.
*MIDI Transformations*
- *Arpeggiate*
-
  - Splits selected notes into arpeggiated notes based on chosen pattern settings, using transposition, steps, rate, and gate controls.
- *Chop*
-
  - Divides selected MIDI notes into up to 64 parts, allowing custom patterns with gaps, emphasis, and random variation in start and end times.
- *Connect*
-
  - Generates new notes to fill gaps between existing notes, with randomized placement controlled by spread, density, rate, and tie parameters.
- *Glissando*
-
  - An MPE tool that connects the pitch of one note to the next along a pitch bend curve, tying them together with adjustable start and curve parameters.
- *LFO*
-
  - An MPE tool that modulates pitch bend, slide, or pressure using a low-frequency oscillator with customizable shape, rate, and amplitude.
- *Ornament*
-
  - Adds flam or grace notes to the beginning of selected notes, with controls for position, velocity, pitch, and chance.
- *Quantize*
-
  - Adjusts the timing of selected notes by moving or stretching them according to specified quantization settings, including grid size or meter values.
- *Recombine*
-
  - Rearranges note parameters (position, pitch, duration, velocity) across a selection using shuffle, mirror, or rotate modes.
- *Span*
-
  - Transforms note durations using legato, tenuto, or staccato articulations, with offset and variation for added control.
- *Strum*
-
  - Adjusts the start times of notes in a chord following a shape defined by strum low, strum high, and tension parameters.
- *Time Warp*
-
  - Transforms selected notes according to a speed curve, enabling tempo variations like accelerando or ritardando.
- *Velocity Shaper*
-
  - A Max for Live tool that shapes the velocities of selected notes using an adjustable envelope with minimum, maximum, loop, and rotate controls.
