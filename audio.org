- Ping-Pong Delay
  - Usable delay effect
  - Not practical around the full range of the pad
  - most usefull near the middle of the pad
- Complex Reverb

  - These "Out" Effects are probably better in the Bar, where you hit it like a button
  - but, this one is good for a fake-out
